How the observatory works
Four passes over every block. No wallet, no keys, no trades — it only watches, reads and reports.

    

      
A · OBSERVE
Watch every launchpad
The scanner follows token factories and new pools across 12 launchpads on Robinhood Chain, block by block.

        
$ observ scan --chain 4663
✓ block 112,605,894 · 3 txs matched
✓ PONS  launch  $SNUMMO
✓ FLAP  pool    $NVDAAI/WETH

Read the contract
Paste a tx hash or token address. The agent reads metadata, holders, creator wallet and runs a swap test.

04
Questions
Short answers. Ask the agent for long ones.

    

      
Is OBSERV free to use?
Yes. The observatory, threat radar, genesis certificates and The 4663 Daily are free and need no sign-up. Agent questions have a fair-use limit per visitor.

      
Is anything here financial advice?
No. OBSERV describes what happened on-chain. It never tells you to buy, sell or hold, and a low threat score does not mean a token is safe.

      
How is the threat score calculated?
Each heuristic signal (sell blocked, creator exit, bundled genesis, top-10 concentration, clone names, fake volume and more) adds a weighted amount, capped at 100. Every signal is shown with the data behind it, and anything that couldn't be checked is listed as not evaluated.

      
Does it connect to my wallet?
Never. OBSERV is read-only: no wallet connection, no signatures, no approvals. If any site asks you to connect a wallet "for OBSERV", it isn't us.

      
Where does the data come from?
Directly from Robinhood Chain RPC (blocks, logs, contract reads) and DexPaprika for pools and market data.

      
Which chains and launchpads are covered?
Robinhood Chain launchpads. Elysium is next on the roadmap, followed by more chains. Open Coverage for the live map.
